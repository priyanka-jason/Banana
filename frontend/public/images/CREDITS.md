# Image credits

## Product pictures

All files in `products/` (and `avocados.webp`, `eggs-v2.webp`, `gala-apples.webp`, `milk.webp`)
are built from **Fluent Emoji** by Microsoft, "Color" style.

- Source: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
- Licence: MIT (free to use, change and share, including commercially; keep the notice below)
- Changes: icons are combined (several copies for bulk sizes, a small corner icon to tell
  similar products apart), some are recoloured, and all are converted to AVIF/WebP. The build
  is in `frontend/design/product-icons/`.

Icons used: Avocado, Bacon, Bagel, Banana, Bell pepper, Beverage box, Blueberries, Bowl with
spoon, Bread, Broccoli, Brown mushroom, Bubbles, Butter, Canned food, Carrot, Cheese wedge,
Chestnut, Chicken, Cooked rice, Cow face, Croissant, Cucumber, Cut of meat, Droplet, Dumpling,
Ear of corn, Egg, Fish, French fries, Glass of milk, Grapes, Green salad, Hot beverage, Ice
cream, Jar, Leafy green, Lemon, Lotion bottle, Oden, Onion, Pea pod, Peanuts, Pig face, Pizza,
Potable water, Potato, Poultry leg, Pouring liquid, Red apple, Roll of paper, Sake, Seedling,
Sheaf of rice, Shrimp, Snowflake, Spaghetti, Sponge, Steaming bowl, Strawberry, Sunflower, Taco,
Tangerine, Tomato.
